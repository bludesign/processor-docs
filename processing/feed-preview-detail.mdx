## Purpose

Feed preview detail shows the content selected for one generated Atom entry, including its primary source, related coverage, dates, links, and normalized body.

## Prerequisites and permissions

Open an entry from [feed preview](/processing/feed-preview). The underlying group and items must still exist.

## Normal workflow

1. Confirm the generated title, author/source, timestamps, and destination link.
2. Review the body that a consumer will receive.
3. Compare related articles when the selection is unexpected.
4. Return to the group or item detail to diagnose configuration.

## Important controls and behavior

- This view is a representation of current data, not a frozen copy of the published entry.
- Primary and longest articles may differ.
- External links are supplied by upstream feeds and are not rewritten for availability.

## Failure states

A previously bookmarked preview can stop resolving after derived state is cleared and rebuilt. Missing content should be checked on the underlying item before treating it as an Atom serialization defect.

## Operational notes

Capture the group and item identifiers when reporting a feed issue; the visual title alone may not uniquely identify a rapidly changing entry.
