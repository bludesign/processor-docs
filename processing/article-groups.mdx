## Purpose

Article groups collect feed items that fall within a folder's semantic and temporal rules. Each group identifies a primary article, the longest available article, and related coverage with scores.

## Prerequisites and permissions

Similarity grouping must be enabled and successfully processed for at least one folder. Authenticated users can browse groups.

## Normal workflow

1. Select a folder.
2. Sort by newest, oldest, or most similar depending on the investigation.
3. Review the primary and longest articles and expand related items.
4. Load more groups or open an item for source-level detail.

## Important controls and behavior

- **Primary** reflects source priority and grouping policy.
- **Longest** identifies the most complete stored body and can differ from primary.
- **Related** items display their similarity to the group anchor.
- Sorting changes presentation only; it does not recalculate groups.

## Failure states

Sparse groups usually mean the similarity threshold is strict, the time window is short, embeddings changed, or feeds did not publish overlapping coverage. Inspect the debugger before clearing state.

## Operational notes

Groups are derived from the selected embedding model. Model changes should be treated as a reindexing event and validated against representative folders.
