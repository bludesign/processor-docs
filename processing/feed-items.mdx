## Purpose

Feed items is the searchable inventory of articles copied from FreshRSS. It is the fastest way to verify that content arrived before investigating embeddings or groups.

## Prerequisites and permissions

At least one folder must be processed. Any authenticated user can browse items they are authorized to view.

## Normal workflow

1. Search title, author, or source.
2. Filter to a folder and optionally hide items already marked read.
3. Load additional results as needed.
4. Open an item to inspect full content, attachments, metadata, and similar items.

## Important controls and behavior

- Folder and unread filters are combined with the search term.
- **Load more** extends the current result set without changing filters.
- Read state originates in FreshRSS and is synchronized; it is not inferred from opening an item in Processor.
- Group membership and similarity are derived state and can change after reprocessing.

## Failure states

An item absent from the list may be outside the configured lookback, belong to a disabled folder, or not have synchronized from FreshRSS. Incomplete titles or bodies reflect upstream content unless processing reports a parsing error.

## Operational notes

Use the stable item detail URL when sharing a diagnosis. Search results and unread totals may change as synchronization continues.
