## Purpose

The detail page shows the normalized record Processor uses for downstream grouping. It separates upstream metadata, stored article content, enclosure data, and similarity relationships.

## Prerequisites and permissions

The item must already exist in Processor. Any authenticated user with normal application access can view it.

## Normal workflow

1. Confirm the source, publication time, author, link, and read state.
2. Review the stored body and attachments for upstream truncation or parsing problems.
3. Inspect the item's group, similarity score, and related candidates.
4. Open the source article when the normalized copy is insufficient.

## Important controls and behavior

- Source links leave Processor and follow the URL supplied by the feed.
- Similarity values are meaningful only relative to the folder's configured model and threshold.
- Attachments represent RSS/Atom enclosures and may be absent for ordinary articles.
- The displayed group can change after clear-and-reprocess operations.

## Failure states

An empty body can be valid for summary-only feeds. A broken source link or attachment is controlled by the publisher. Missing similarity data usually means grouping is disabled, processing is pending, or embedding generation failed.

## Operational notes

When diagnosing grouping quality, compare both the primary and longest article rather than assuming they are the same record.
