Processor is a self-hosted dashboard that reads subscriptions from FreshRSS, generates article embeddings with Ollama, stores and searches them in Qdrant, groups similar coverage, and publishes delayed grouped Atom feeds. You keep the application, SQLite database, model, and vector store under your control.

## How it fits together

1. FreshRSS supplies subscriptions, folders, article bodies, and unread state.
2. Ollama converts the weighted title and article content into an embedding.
3. Qdrant stores the vector plus normalized article metadata.
4. Processor compares nearby vectors, records durable groups and match history in SQLite, and marks completed FreshRSS items.
5. Signed grouped Atom feeds expose the longest article in each group, with related entries linked from it.
