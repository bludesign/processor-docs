## Purpose

WebSub operations exposes Processor's hub state: active subscriptions, pending verification, queued publications, deliveries, publication history, and content snapshots.

## Prerequisites and permissions

WebSub must be configured with externally reachable callback/topic URLs. The diagnostics page requires authenticated administrative access.

## Normal workflow

1. Confirm subscriptions are active and verification is not pending unexpectedly.
2. Inspect the publish queue after grouped feeds change.
3. Review delivery attempts, status codes, and retry state.
4. Compare a publication with its snapshot when consumers report stale content.
5. Use endpoint and application logs to correlate a failed operation.

## Important controls and behavior

- Processor identifies itself as `processor-websub/1.0` for WebSub client requests.
- Verification and delivery are asynchronous and can be retried.
- Diagnostic payloads are bounded and secrets are redacted before display.
- Queue visibility is operational state, not a promise of durable long-term history.

## Failure states

Pending verification commonly indicates an unreachable callback or a mismatched verification token. Delivery failures may be subscriber timeouts, non-success HTTP responses, DNS/TLS problems, or an invalid topic URL. Repeated failures should be diagnosed before forcing publication again.

## Operational notes

Deployments behind a reverse proxy must preserve callback paths, query parameters, and public scheme/host. Monitor queue age and repeated delivery attempts to prevent slow consumers from hiding broader publication problems.
