## Purpose

Posts is a small authenticated content-management surface used to demonstrate and verify GraphQL list, filter, create, update, publish/draft, and delete behavior.

## Prerequisites and permissions

You must be signed in. Available mutations are still governed by GraphQL authorization rules.

## Normal workflow

1. Search or filter existing posts.
2. Create a draft with a title and body.
3. Review and update the record.
4. Publish it when ready, or return it to draft.
5. Delete records that are no longer required.

## Important controls and behavior

- Filtering changes the list only.
- Draft/publication state is stored explicitly.
- Mutations are executed through the same typed GraphQL client/code-generation path used elsewhere in Processor.

## Failure states

Validation errors block incomplete input. Unauthorized mutations indicate session or role enforcement. A list that appears stale should be refreshed after confirming the mutation completed.

## Operational notes

Posts are independent from RSS feed items and grouped Atom output. Do not use this feature as a substitute for upstream feed content management.
